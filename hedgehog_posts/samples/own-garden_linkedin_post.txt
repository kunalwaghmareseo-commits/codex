I stopped checking what my peers were posting for 30 days. My work got better.

Every scroll was a quiet scoreboard: their promotion, their launch, their raise.

None of it changed my results. It only changed my mood.

What helped:
• Measure yourself against last month's you
• Celebrate a peer's win, then close the tab
• Spend that energy on the one thing only you can grow

Your garden needs your attention, not your neighbour's fence.
