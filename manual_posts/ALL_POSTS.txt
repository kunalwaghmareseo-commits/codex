POST 01  |  folder: 01_own-garden
QUOTE ON IMAGE: Comparison steals the joy from your own garden.
========================================================================

---- LINKEDIN (Quiamo page) ---- image: linkedin_3x4.jpg

I stopped checking what my peers were posting for 30 days. My work got better.

Every scroll was a quiet scoreboard: their promotion, their launch, their raise.

None of it changed my results. It only changed my mood.

What helped:
• Measure yourself against last month's you
• Celebrate a peer's win, then close the tab
• Spend that energy on the one thing only you can grow

Your garden needs your attention, not your neighbour's fence.

QUOTE ON IMAGE: Praise in public. Correct in private. Care always.
========================================================================

---- LINKEDIN (Quiamo page) ---- image: linkedin_3x4.jpg

The 6-word management rule I wish I'd learned in my first year.

Praise in public. Correct in private. Care always.

Public praise builds confidence across the whole team.
Private correction protects dignity, and people actually listen.
Care is what makes both of them land.

Skip the third part and the first two feel like tactics.
